**1.1 Background of the Study**

Computer retail shops play an important role in assisting customers who
want to purchase personalized or custom-built PCs according to their needs
and budget. Many customers visit these shops to request custom-built
computers tailored to their specific requirements, such as gaming,
professional work, or general-purpose computing. During this process,
customers often rely on the knowledge and expertise of the shop’s staff to
help determine which hardware components should be used in the build.
Because many customers are not familiar with computer hardware
specifications and compatibility requirements, they frequently ask
technicians for guidance in selecting appropriate components.

In retail establishments like Brandcom the experience and expertise of
technicians largely dictate the recommendations they will offer for custom
PCs. Technicians will usually begin the initial evaluation with some general

you intend to use the computer and what special features you would like
the computer to have. Once the technicians have completed this initial
evaluation, there is often a fairly large amount of manual effort involved in
the early stages; it is essential that the technician validate the hardware
compatibility, examine the information on all of the different specifications
for the PC components and analyze multiple options for the various PC
components to ensure that the finished product will create a cohesive unit.

But the fast-paced evolution of computer hardware has brought a huge
assortment of components, each with its own performance and
compatibility quirks. Consequently, figuring out the best mix of parts for a
custom PC can get pretty complicated and eat up a lot of time. Plus,
customers sometimes want several build options at different price points,
which adds even more time to the process of putting together
recommendations.

Because of these challenges, there is a growing need for a system that can
assist technicians and customers in selecting compatible and optimized PC
configurations more efficiently. A decision support system that utilizes
machine learning techniques can help analyze user requirements and

generate custom PC builds according to the user's budget, intended use
and component preferences.

The goal of such a system is to support computer retail shops (e.g.
Brandcom) today to deliver faster, more consistent and accurate PC build
recommendations to their customers.

**Delimitations of the Study**

The proposed system focuses on recommending compatible PC builds and
improving the consultation process for computer retail shops. However, it
has the following boundaries:

1. **Exclusively for Custom PC Builds:** The system is only intended for
   recommending custom PC configurations and does not manage other retail
   operations, such as inventory or sales tracking.
2. **Machine Learning Dataset Limitations:** Recommendations are
   based on the dataset of hardware components included during system
   development and may not include very recent or uncommon components.

2.1 Research Design**

The study utilizes a developmental and descriptive research design to
design, develop and evaluate SmartBuild, an AI/ML-based decision support
system for custom PC build recommendation.

The developmental research design is employed in developing the system
itself, which involves the creation of the database design, implementation
of the random forest algorithm, and development of the user interface. On
the other hand, the descriptive research design is employed in evaluating
the performance of the developed system in providing compatible and
optimized PC builds based on user inputs.

The process of creating the decision support system entails five steps,
namely data gathering, data preparation, model building, system
implementation, and evaluation. The final product should be able to cater
to the needs of computer retail shops such as BrandCom.

**2.2 Locale of the Study**

The study is conducted at BrandCom Computer and Repair Shop, serving

customers.

SmartBuild system.
